Figure 1. Systematic Softmax evaluation of a probability-preserving FSM--TT reference. (a) Dimension sweep at N=4,r=8; each dimension has a separately trained model. (b) Rank sweep; the TT+exact-Softmax audit control retains runtime Softmax and is not a Softmax replacement. (c) FSM-state sweep. (d) Finite-window RMSE to FP32 Softmax with 64 extra burn-in cycles; the probability-averaging estimator is a diagnostic, not emitted categorical frequencies. The IID categorical curve uses exact target probabilities and is a sampling reference, not an implemented accelerator. (e) Distribution stress tests, including input-clipping effects. (f) Common-shift invariance without clipping. Points/error bars show means/sample SD over five model initializations; stream repetitions are first averaged within each model. Main tests use 8,192 rows; stress/invariance tests use 4,096; stream tests use 128 rows and eight stream repetitions per model.

Figure 2. Sampling and implementation diagnostics. (a) State-only versus state-plus-output sampling under ideal stationary initialization. (b) Center reset with zero/64 burn-in cycles versus ideal stationary initialization. (c) Probability of sharing a uniform RNG draw across input dimensions: this is not the Pearson correlation coefficient. Three model seeds and four stream repetitions per seed are used; burn-in=256. (d) Offline probability-grid projection with step 2^-B; each core row remains normalized by largest-remainder count allocation. B is probability resolution, not a complete hardware storage-width specification. Dotted lines show unquantized FP32-core error. TT contractions are evaluated in FP32 throughout the bitstream simulations.
