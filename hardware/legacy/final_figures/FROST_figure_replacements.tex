\begin{figure}[!htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{Images/FROST_hardware_stage3.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{FPGA resource efficiency of 64-sample attention.}
\textbf{a--c}, Absolute post-route counts of combinational resources
(COMB), digital signal processing (DSP) blocks and block random-access
memory (BRAM) instances for the three arithmetic controls and optimized
FROST at $L=64$. FROST uses 73.3\% fewer COMB resources than the
four-lane FP32 control and no DSP or BRAM instances. Counts include
support logic and alternative storage implementations; values are
stored in distributed memory and constant CDFs are implemented as logic.
Colours identify implementations consistently across panels, with FROST
highlighted in coral. Resource axes are independent and start at zero.
The complete resource counts, including flip-flops, are reported in
Table~\ref{tab:fused_fpga_resources}.}
\label{fig:fused_hardware_stage3}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[!htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{Images/FROST_hardware_tradeoffs.pdf}
\caption{\textbf{Lower energy and a favourable area--power operating point.}
\textbf{a}, Estimated energy per 16-query attention tile for the three
arithmetic controls and optimized FROST at $L=64$. Coral percentages
indicate FROST's energy reduction relative to the control in the same
row: 70.7\% against single-lane FP32, 36.0\% against four-lane FP32
and 20.8\% against four-lane FX16. Reductions are calculated from
unrounded results; the energy axis starts at zero.
\textbf{b}, Mapped standard-cell area versus estimated active power
for the same implementations. FROST occupies the lowest-area,
lowest-power point among these evaluated kernels. Both panels use
Nangate45 at 1.10 V, $25\,^{\circ}$C and 15 MHz. FROST power and energy
are means across three stream seeds on one activity tile; error bars
show sample standard deviations and may be smaller than the marks.
All power and energy values are pre-layout tool estimates.}
\label{fig:fused_hardware_tradeoffs}
\end{figure}
